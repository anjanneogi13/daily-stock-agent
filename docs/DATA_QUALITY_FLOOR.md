# Data Quality Floor

**Constant**: `src.data_quality.DATA_QUALITY_FLOOR = 2026-05-02`

## What it is
The earliest `pick_date` for which all current safety gates were active.
Picks before this date are **fossils** — generated by a less-protected
agent — and should be excluded from any outcome / win-rate analysis.

## Why
By May 4 2026, several gates had been added that did not exist on
Apr 28 / 29 / May 1:

| Gate | Wired on | What it prevents |
|---|---|---|
| apply_sector_cap (max 2/sector) | Apr 30 | 16-SEMI concentration disasters |
| apply_tag_cap (max 2/tag) | Apr 30 | tag-level concentration (SEMI/AI) |
| apply_hard_blocks (penny + SL buffer) | May 2 | $1.66 penny stocks (SLNH) |
| BUG-5 tiered SL minimums | May 2 | mega-cap stops too tight |

The Apr 28 -> May 1 picks_log includes 9 closed picks. **All 9 lost.**
But 7 of those losses were a single SEMI sector crash (concentration
bug) and 1 was a penny-stock loss (penny-block bug). These losses
**cannot recur** because the gates that would prevent them are now in
place. Counting them in win-rate stats would slander the current agent.

## Usage
    from src.data_quality import is_above_floor, filter_to_quality
    clean = filter_to_quality(all_picks)

## When to bump the floor
Bump DATA_QUALITY_FLOOR whenever a STRUCTURAL safety gate is added or
substantially changed.

DO bump for:
  - New filter that drops picks
  - Position sizing logic changes
  - New hard block

DO NOT bump for:
  - Cosmetic changes
  - Score weight tuning
  - Bucket boundary tweaks (signal calibration, not gates)

When you bump, archive the old floor in this file's history table.
