## 🎯 The product (founder framing)

We are NOT building a stock-pick bot. We are building a **trusted US stock
research product** with 6 pillars:

1. **Daily premarket picks** (suggestion-only, never trades)
2. **Learning loop** that runs nightly/weekly/quarterly/yearly
3. **Self-calibration** of weights, thresholds, kill-list, patterns
4. **Telegram communication** scheduled (daily / weekly / monthly / yearly reports)
5. **On-demand Q&A** (`/why NVDA`, `/status`, `/health`, `/funnel`, `/lessons`, `/perf`, `/xray`)
6. **Performance reports & x-ray** with SPY benchmark comparison

Trust is the product. Trust before automation. Trust before monetization.

User does not code. Always provide ONE safe copy-paste terminal block.
